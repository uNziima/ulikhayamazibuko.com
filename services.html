How the process works.

Payment structure

50% deposit before development starts, this reserves the build slot and covers domain and hosting setup. 50% on completion before files, credentials or access are released.

Content deadline

Client content which includes text and images must arrive within 14 days of the deposit. If content doesn't arrive in time the project is paused.

Revision policy

A revision is a change to existing content and not a new page or new feature. Minor text or image corrections don't count against revision rounds. Anything new is a change order, quoted before work starts.

Domain ownership

Your domain is always registered in your name. UM Freelance manages it under mandate but never owns it. Renewal is invoiced 30 days before expiry, nothing renews without your approval.

Hosting

Free for the first month. From month two: R150–250/month. This covers the hosting cost, renewal administration, and first point of contact if something breaks.

Post-launch warranty

14 days after launch covers bug fixes only which is the system failing to do what was agreed. Anything not in the original specification is new development, quoted separately.
