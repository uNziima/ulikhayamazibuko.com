Case Study 01

South African SMEs get enterprise-grade business intelligence at a price built for small business.

The Problem

Most small business owners in South Africa are making significant financial and operational decisions without reliable visibility into their own performance. Not because they are careless but because the infrastructure to see clearly has never been accessible at their price point.

A business owner knows roughly what came in this week but cannot determine whether this week was structurally better or worse than the same week last year. They have a sense of which products move but cannot confidently identify which ones generate the most margin. Cash flow tightens and they cannot pinpoint when the trend started or what drove it.

The result is a business that is functional but perpetually reactive. Problems are noticed after they have already developed. Opportunities are identified too late. Decisions that could be data-driven are instinct-driven, not by choice, but by default.

Business Clarity closes that gap.

The Approach

01
Client-owned data architecture The Google Sheet that feeds the dashboard lives in the client's Google Drive, not ours. All data, reports and dashboard assets are owned by the client from the moment they are created. POPIA-aligned by design.
02
Live performance dashboard A Google Looker Studio dashboard built for each client's business, accessible on any device via one bookmarked link. 5–7 core metrics selected at onboarding and not generic KPIs, but what actually drives that business.
03
Automated monthly intelligence On the last day of every month, the system automatically generates a one-page Decision Brief in plain language including the numbers that mattered most, one thing to stop, one thing to start, one watch item.
04
Quarterly strategic review Every three months, an automated Quarterly Business Review covers the full quarter's performance, three wins, three risks or opportunities, and recommended priorities for the next 90 days.

What Clients Get

90s Dashboard designed for a 90-second morning check with the state of business visible instantly
100% Client data ownership and can cancel anytime, everything stays in their Google account
0 Manual effort required from the client once the system is configured and running

Case Study 02

A Newcastle delivery business runs its full order-to-delivery pipeline on WhatsApp with automated, real-time, zero manual steps.

The Problem

The business was taking orders through WhatsApp but everything after that was manual. Orders tracked in a spreadsheet someone updated by hand. Drivers given verbal instructions or forwarded messages. Payment reconciliation tallied at end of day.

One missed message and an order fell through. One late update and a driver went to the wrong address. One distracted moment and the day's cash didn't balance. The business was growing but the process couldn't grow with it.

The ask was simple: make the system work the way the business already worked on WhatsApp but without any of the manual steps.

What Was Built

01
Two-track WhatsApp ordering system Customers order directly in WhatsApp. The backend intercepts the message, validates it, confirms the order and routes it automatically. No one types anything into a spreadsheet.
02
GPS location capture via WhatsApp Customers share their location once in WhatsApp. GPS coordinates are captured via webhook, saved to Sheets and rendered as a pinpoint on the driver's live map which tied to the exact order.
03
Automated delivery broadcasts "On the way", "we're outside", delivery confirmation and payment confirmation all fire automatically from backend events. No one sends a message manually during a delivery run.
04
Driver-facing Progressive Web App Runs on any Android or iOS device via a private-key-gated link. Colour-coded map pins, optimised route, live delivery tracking, proof of delivery, payment collection and cash reconciliation which is all ran from a phone in a moving vehicle.

What Changed

0 Manual data entry steps during a full delivery run from start to finish
Live Driver visibility with real map pins replace verbal instructions and forwarded messages
Daily A real business depends on this system which is deployed on Railway, running in production
